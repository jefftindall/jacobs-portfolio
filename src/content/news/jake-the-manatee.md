On May 29, 2025, during [CMA Research Institute](https://mission.cmaquarium.org/research-institute/manatee-research/belize-manatee-conservation/) manatee health assessments in Belize, the team named a male manatee **Jake**.

That day Jacob helped collect a urine sample, assisted with blood sampling, and kept the animal wet while others took a tissue sample from the paddle and placed a tag under the skin. [Jamal Galves](https://www.neaq.org/person/jamal-galves/) was on the boat.

The full expedition notes — including Todd at more than 860 lb a week later — are on [Belize 2025](/conservation/belize-2025).
