On May 25, 2026, the Belize manatee team started the day with dolphins near the boat, then completed health assessments on two males.

Jacob field-tested a **manatee breath-tracker app he built**. It logs each breath’s time and an average breaths-per-minute rate during an exam. He planned to adjust it at home for aquariums and conservationists. This site does not claim it is listed in an app store.

Later that week the team assessed **Celeshia**, a female of about 903 lb, and Jacob dived with [Glenford Guy](https://www.cmaquarium.org/).
