In July 2026, Jacob spent two weeks volunteering at [Clearwater Marine Aquarium](https://www.cmaquarium.org/) — Guest Engagement and the Dive Team. He learned animal identification and daily habitat care. Staff invited him back when he is in town.

The same Clearwater stretch included time with [Jamal Galves](https://www.neaq.org/person/jamal-galves/) at the [Buddy Powell Manatee Rehabilitation Center](https://www.cmaquarium.org/manatee-rehabilitation-center/), and a tour of **Eckerd College**, the marine-science school Jacob hopes to attend.
