In July 2026, Jacob spent two weeks as a volunteer at [Clearwater Marine Aquarium](https://www.cmaquarium.org/) — his first stretch on staff-side work after years as a marine science camper.

He worked in **Guest Engagement** and on the **Dive Team**. Guest Engagement meant learning how the aquarium identifies individual animals and how daily care actually runs, not only how exhibits look from the public side. The Dive Team put him in the water.

On dive days he suited up in the tank room — wetsuit, regulator, tanks on the rack — then went into habitats to clean glass and rockwork from the inside. Staff invited him back when he is in town.

During that Clearwater stretch he also stood with [Jamal Galves](https://www.neaq.org/person/jamal-galves/) at the [Dr. James “Buddy” Powell Manatee Rehabilitation Center](https://www.cmaquarium.org/manatee-rehabilitation-center/) — the building CMA named for the scientist Jacob met in 2024.

Away from the aquarium he paddleboarded mangrove shoreline and watched a manatee pass under the board — the same animals he had helped assess in Belize, this time swimming on their own.

<figure>
  <img src="/images/conservation/paddleboard-manatee.jpg" alt="Jacob on a paddleboard in mangrove water with a manatee visible just under the surface" width="1536" height="2048" />

The same visit included a tour of **Eckerd College** in St. Petersburg. Eckerd’s marine science program, and a campus on the water, is the college Jacob hopes to attend.

The field seasons that led here are [Belize 2025](/conservation/belize-2025) and [Belize 2026](/conservation/belize-2026). The app he tested on the boats is [The manatee breath tracker](/conservation/breath-tracker).
