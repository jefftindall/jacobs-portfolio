Jacob Tindall cares about **marine mammal conservation**, especially dolphins and manatees, and about **music** — piano and original pieces.

He is from the **Cartersville**, Georgia area. Summer marine science camp at [Clearwater Marine Aquarium](https://www.cmaquarium.org/) is where a friendship with **Nicholas**, a rescue dolphin, took hold. [Tampa Bay 28](https://www.tampabay28.com/news/region-pinellas/georgia-boy-enrolled-in-clearwater-summer-camp-befriends-dolphin) shared that story. Aquarium staff later pointed to it as an example of how meeting animals in care can inspire future biologists, trainers, veterinarians, and advocates.

In June 2024, when he was 11, he met manatee conservationists [Jamal Galves](https://www.neaq.org/person/jamal-galves/), [Dr. James “Buddy” Powell](https://www.cmaquarium.org/manatee-rehabilitation-center/), and [Tanya Ward](https://www.cmaquarium.org/manatee-rehabilitation-center/) at CMA’s Echoes of the Ocean speaker night. Jamal was also 11 when he first met Dr. Powell. Jamal invited Jacob to join Belize manatee work. That turning point is on [Meeting conservation heroes](/conservation/meeting-heroes).

He then joined [CMA Research Institute](https://mission.cmaquarium.org/research-institute/manatee-research/belize-manatee-conservation/) health assessments in Belize in 2025 and 2026 — including a manatee named **Jake**, breath monitoring, blood and urine sampling, and tagging. The field notes are [Belize 2025](/conservation/belize-2025) and [Belize 2026](/conservation/belize-2026). After the first expedition he spoke to the **Rotary Club of Etowah** as an aspiring marine biologist.

In July 2026 he volunteered at CMA in Guest Engagement and on the Dive Team, and toured **Eckerd College**, the marine-science school he hopes to attend. That chapter is on [Volunteering at CMA](/conservation/volunteering).

He plays piano. His first recital was at **ACT I** in Cartersville. Listen to later recordings on [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/jacob-tindall-official) or the [music](/music) page. Photos live in the [gallery](/gallery).

In spare time he also likes games like Fortnite and Minecraft. This site does not list usernames, friend lists, or Discord.
